Self-review

  • Sales-pitch check. Cut "savings" everywhere; the interest difference is always "the gap a family rate closes." Removed any line that framed the loan as good for Steve and Dawn financially. The only benefit named for them is family across the street.
  • Honesty check. Scene 5 and slide 2 both say the payment still rises by about $686 a month at the family rate. Scene 6 says plainly that they could earn more elsewhere.
  • Easy to say no. "The home they hope to build" throughout, never "when we move in." Scene 8 and slide 5 say no answer is needed tonight, and Amber's note on slide 5 says "not yet" is okay.
  • Rate never fixed. 3% is called the example in scenes 5 and 6 and on slide 1; scene 6 says the actual rate is theirs to decide.
  • Banned words. Automated search of the script and deck found no em or en dashes, no "guarantee," and none of AFR, security deed, Form 709, imputed interest, or amortization. "Attorney and CPA" appears once in the video and once in the deck.
  • Siblings. Appear only in appendix A1.
  • Nothing invented about the home. The house is only ever "the home," plus the sports barn as the brief describes it. The illustrations show a house outline, the barn with the listed equipment, a family table, and the street.
  • Pace. Scene 5 is the longest at about 60 seconds and carries five numbers. I kept it because it is the whole argument; everything else is under 50 seconds.
